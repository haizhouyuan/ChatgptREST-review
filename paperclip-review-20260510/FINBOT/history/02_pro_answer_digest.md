2. KOL/blogger/Fu Zong material is D-tier interpretive input until claim-specific primary corroboration exists.
3. Connector smoke or route proof is not claim corroboration.
4. Blocked fetches are FetchAttempt/EvidenceGap records, never EvidenceItem records.
5. SignalWindow means research review window, not trade signal.
6. Validator pass must cover real downstream artifacts and negative fixtures.
7. Memory is no-write/candidate-delta only unless Governance approves authority promotion.
