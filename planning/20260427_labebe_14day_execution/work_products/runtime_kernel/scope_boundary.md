# Scope Boundary v0

Issue: `LAB-001`
Status: Draft

## DTC Website Prototype

Purpose:

- consumer-facing Labebe independent-site replacement prototype.

Allowed:

- homepage;
- collection pages;
- PDP;
- gift path;
- room/bundle path;
- trust modules;
- mobile-first shopping flow;
- product gallery and media modules;
- cart/bundle prototype if needed.

Forbidden:

- AI Studio;
- Paperclip board;
- internal product matrix;
- claim/evidence ledger visible to consumers;
- unsupported reviews, ratings, certifications, safety/material claims, market demand claims or development-benefit claims;
- fake founder story, fake awards, fake press, fake manufacturing origin;
- design that works only as a generic children's brand template.

## Boss Gallery / AI Wow Demo

Purpose:

- decision-maker-facing result-first demonstration of AI business/product applications.

Allowed:

- product opportunity radar;
- VOC to concept;
- concept-to-market asset matrix;
- claim gate;
- evidence labels;
- short-video/storyboard concepts;
- executive walkthrough.

Forbidden:

- presenting AI concepts as production-ready CAD;
- claiming certified safety, demand, lower cost or launch readiness without evidence;
- placing Boss Gallery inside DTC consumer navigation;
- opening with Paperclip process rather than Labebe business result.

## Shared Evidence Layer

Allowed:

- product master;
- PDP facts;
- Amazon/marketplace identity sample;
- public media asset manifests;
- claim ledger;
- design reference mapping;
- Commerce Decision Layer artifacts.

Forbidden:

- using unknown fields as final copy;
- treating scraped/public media as legal clearance;
- using Amazon VOC before ASIN identity confidence.
