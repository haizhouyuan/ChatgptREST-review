Description

🌟 Multifunctional Fun

This baby push walker has lots of fun learning toys like a xylophone, guiro, bead maze, shape sorter, rotating blocks, and colorful beads. These help babies explore their senses, build hand-eye coordination, and practice problem-solving skills.




🚶 Adjustable Speed

The walker has a speed control system so you can easily adjust how fast it moves. This helps create a safe learning space for toddlers as they take their first steps.




✅ Safe Materials

Made from solid wood and MDF with smooth rounded edges to protect little hands. All materials and paints meet strict safety standards (ASTM/CE in the USA and EN71 CE in Europe).




🧸 Thoughtful Design

Quiet rubber wheels won’t scratch floors. There’s also a large storage space in the back for toys, so your baby can bring favorites along on adventures.




🎁 Perfect Gift for Babies

The cute design makes this a great gift for boys and girls aged 1 year and up. It can also be used as a Montessori toy as your child grows.

Specifications
Age Suitability	

Recommended for children ages 1–3 years (12M+).


Materials	

Solid wood and MDF frame with smooth, rounded edges; non-toxic water-based paint; durable rubber-rimmed wheels for smooth, quiet rolling.


Dimensions & Weight	

Overall Size: 13.8″ L × 13″ W × 18.5″ H (35.0 × 33.0 × 47.0 cm)
Weight: approx. 7.5 lbs (3.4 kg)


Assembly	

Approx. 5-10 minutes by an adult using a household screwdriver.

📃 Download User Manual (PDF)


Care & Maintenance	

Wipe clean with a soft damp cloth; air dry.
Periodically check that all screws remain tight and wheels move smoothly.
