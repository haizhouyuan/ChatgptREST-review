Description

Magical Unicorn Adventure
With her silver horn, pink mane, and soft plush body, this unicorn rocker brings a touch of wonder to playtime. More than a toy, she becomes a gentle friend who invites little riders on magical adventures.




Thoughtful French Design
Created by labebe’s French design team, every detail balances beauty and practicality. The soft cushioned seat with three-sided support and safety belt keeps little ones snug, while the charming look adds a delightful accent to any nursery or playroom.




Safe & Sturdy Build
Crafted with a solid wood base and durable MDF components, this rocker offers smooth, gentle motion and reliable stability. Plush upholstery with neatly stitched PP cotton ensures softness, comfort, and long-lasting use.




A Gift to Treasure
Perfect for children ages 1–3, this unicorn rocker strengthens balance, coordination, and core muscles while sparking imagination. A timeless gift for birthdays, holidays, or everyday play that will be cherished for years.

Safety

WARNING! Not suitable for children under 6 months.




This product may contain small parts and sharp points prior to and during assembly. Adult assembly is required, and children must be kept away until assembly is complete.

Always use under adult supervision. Never leave your child unattended while riding. Use only on flat, horizontal ground and never on steps, slopes, or near stairs, pools, or open flames.

Check regularly that all screws are tight and parts remain secure. Do not use if any components are missing or damaged. This toy is designed for one child at a time.

Please read all safety instructions before use and retain the user manual for future reference. Remove and responsibly recycle all packaging materials before giving the product to a child.
