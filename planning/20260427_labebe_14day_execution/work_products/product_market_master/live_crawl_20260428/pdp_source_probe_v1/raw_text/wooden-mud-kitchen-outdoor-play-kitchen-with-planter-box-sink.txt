Description

Imaginative Outdoor Play
This upgraded mud kitchen invites kids into immersive role play. With a running faucet, detachable sink, stovetops, and a blackboard for “menus,” little chefs can mix, wash, and create their own mud recipes. The added planter box lets them plant seeds, watch roots grow, and explore nature hands-on.




Smart Design for Joyful Play
Featuring shelves, hooks, cooking accessories, and a large transparent planter box, everything has its place. Kids can stir in pots, dig with shovels, or water seedlings—combining gardening and pretend cooking for hours of fun. Designed for backyards and gardens, it’s perfect for shared adventures.




Safe, Durable, Trusted
Made from solid fir wood with water-based, BPA-free paint, this kitchen is built for lasting outdoor use. Smooth surfaces, rounded edges, and compliance with ASTM & CPSIA safety standards ensure worry-free play.




A Gift That Grows with Kids
Ideal for ages 3–8, it sparks creativity, teamwork, and curiosity about nature. A thoughtful gift for birthdays, holidays, or everyday play—bringing kids closer to both imagination and the outdoors.
