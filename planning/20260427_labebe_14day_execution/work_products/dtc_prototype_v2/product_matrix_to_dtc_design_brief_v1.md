# Product Matrix To DTC Design Brief v1

Date: 2026-04-28

## Purpose

This brief converts the product fact matrix into website design direction. It is for the pure Labebe DTC replacement site only. It must not include Paperclip, AI Growth Studio, claim gates, product matrices, or internal automation demos in consumer-facing pages.

## Product Truth Baseline

Current live probe:

- 60 visible Labebe product slugs.
- 46 core products.
- 9 site-all-only rows needing category confirmation.
- 5 regional variants that should not become US homepage heroes.

Product-world split from `labebe_product_strategy_matrix_v1.csv`:

| World | Count | Current evidence posture |
|---|---:|---|
| First Steps & Activity | 17 | Real DTC product family, but Amazon evidence is variant-clustered. |
| Giftable Rockers | 13 | Strongest proof-rich category; seven identity-whitelist candidates include multiple Amazon social-proof samples. |
| Montessori Home & Study | 13 | Important parent-utility category; needs PDP/spec recrawl before strong claims. |
| Pretend Play Worlds | 10 | Cream Wooden Play Kitchen is the current anchor; black/gray kitchen variants must remain separate. |
| Playroom Reset | 7 | Strong for room-planning UX and bundles; marketplace evidence is thin. |

## Homepage Hierarchy

The homepage should be product-led, not generic Montessori-led.

Recommended order:

1. Hero: Giftable Rockers as the emotional/proof-rich entry.
2. Finder: Shop by age, room, and occasion.
3. Product Worlds:
   - Giftable Rockers
   - First Steps & Activity
   - Pretend Play Worlds
   - Montessori Home & Study
   - Playroom Reset
4. Proof-rich products:
   - Pink Unicorn Plush Rocker
   - Crocodile Plush Rocker
   - White Swan Plush Rocker
   - Llama Plush Rocker
   - Fox Plush Rocker
   - Blue Squirrel Plush Rocker
   - Cream Wooden Play Kitchen
5. Room solution band:
   - nursery gift corner
   - toddler movement zone
   - pretend kitchen corner
   - study/art corner
   - storage/reset wall
6. PDP story teaser:
   - "See how one product fits the room"
   - no AI/demo language

## Navigation

Primary nav should reflect buyer mental models:

- Shop by Age
- Shop by Room
- Gifts
- Rockers
- Pretend Play
- Montessori & Study
- Storage

Avoid making "All Products" the primary shopping experience. The catalog is too varied; a flat grid makes the brand feel weaker.

`pink-unicorn-plush-rocker`

Reason:

- Highest DTC visible review count in current probe.
- Strong Amazon PDP sample after identity whitelist.
- Visually distinctive.
- Easy first-birthday / nursery / grandparent-gift story.

Secondary hero candidates:

- `crocodile-plush-rocker`
- `white-swan-plush-rocker`

- `fox-plush-rocker`

Hero should not be only a warm empty room. It needs a recognizable product in the first viewport.

## Collection Treatment

### Giftable Rockers

Role: emotional proof and gift conversion.

Use:

- character-led grid;
- birthday / nursery / grandparent gift filters;
- age badges;
- texture closeups;
- "compare characters" module.

Do not:

- claim Amazon ratings publicly unless approved;
- mix wrong ASIN evidence into Highlander/Yak/Sheep.

### First Steps & Activity

Role: new DTC product family and early-development shopping world.

Use:

- product-family comparison;
- "walker style" filters;
- activity cube / busy board / easel / spinner supporting products;
- clear DTC source only.

Do not:

- collapse Amazon variant clusters into exact product proof;
- overclaim safety or developmental benefit.

### Pretend Play Worlds

Role: large-format play scenes and PDP depth.

Anchor:

- Cream Wooden Play Kitchen.

Use:

- kitchen / coffee shop / bakery / laundry / mud kitchen visual worlds;
- room-scale photography;
- bundle ideas.

Do not:

- map Midnight Serenity or Stylish Kitchen to the Cream Kitchen ASIN.

### Montessori Home & Study

Role: parent utility, daily routine, study/art corner.

Use:

- learning tower variants;
- desk/chair;
- art table/easel;
- room-based shopping.

Do not:

- use the weak Learning Tower Amazon PDP as proof yet.

### Playroom Reset

Role: room order and bundle builder.

Use:

- storage organizer;
- Montessori shelf;
- rotating bookshelf;
- bins/cabinets/stools.

Do not:

- make it look like generic furniture retail. It should explain room outcomes.

## PDP Template Priorities

PDPs should be product-specific, not one generic template.

Required modules:

1. product gallery with recognizable first image;
2. title, price, DTC review count if visible;
3. age / room / occasion chips;
4. product story block;
5. "fits this room" module;
6. dimensions / materials / care only after source capture;
7. bundle suggestions;
8. FAQ with sourced or clearly conservative wording.

Current PDP priority order:

1. Pink Unicorn Plush Rocker.
2. Cream Wooden Play Kitchen.
3. Natural Wood Montessori Shelf or Kids Toy Storage Organizer.
4. Activity Cube / First Steps family page.
5. Learning Tower only after PDP/spec recrawl.

## Visual Style Direction

The site should feel like a better Labebe, not like a generic AI-designed toy shop.

Recommended style:

- editorial product-first layout;
- large product cutouts mixed with real room scenes;
- warm but not beige-only;
- product-world color accents:
  - Rockers: blush, berry, sky, warm oak;
  - First Steps: butter, mint, toy-block primary accents;
  - Pretend Play: cream, tomato, brass, oak;
  - Montessori & Study: sage, chalk, paper white;
  - Playroom Reset: clay, natural wood, ink.
- dense enough for shopping; not a sparse portfolio page.

## What To Avoid

- A flat all-products grid as the main site idea.
- A generic Montessori positioning that ignores the plush rocker proof base.
- A beige-only palette.
- AI/Paperclip/internal demo sections inside the DTC site.
- Fake reviews, fake certifications, fake awards, fake origin claims.
- Amazon search-page data as public proof.
- Variant-cluster ASINs as exact SKU evidence.

## Next Design Iteration Requirement

The next visual redesign should present at least three materially different product-backed directions:

1. Gift Theater: character-forward, emotional, high-conversion gift shop.
2. Room Builder: parent utility, shop by room/age, bundles and order.
3. Play Worlds: cinematic pretend-play and activity zones with richer product storytelling.

All three should use the same product truth layer but differ in layout, color, rhythm, typography, merchandising hierarchy, and motion. The decision should be based on buyer clarity and product conversion, not novelty alone.
