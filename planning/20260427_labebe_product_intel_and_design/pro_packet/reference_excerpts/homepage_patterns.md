# Homepage Patterns
- **Hero**: Warm home scene + specific age/gift CTA (from Lalo/Nestig/Lovevery).
- **Shop by Age**: 6–18m, 18–36m, 3–6y, 6y+ (from Lovevery).
- **Shop by Room**: Nursery, Playroom, Kitchen, Bedroom, Outdoor (from Crate & Kids/Pottery Barn Kids).
- **Four Worlds**: Giftable Rockers, Montessori at Home, Tiny Pretend Worlds, Playroom Reset (from Milton & Goose/Tender Leaf).
- **Best Sellers**: Grid with real prices and review counts.
- **Watch It in Action**: 15s video clips per major SKU (TikTok/PDP integration).
- **Trust & Safety**: Safety, materials, testing, age-appropriate info (from Lovevery/Little Partners).
- **AI Growth Studio Teaser**: Visualizing one SKU turning into multi-channel assets (from Linear/Raycast).
