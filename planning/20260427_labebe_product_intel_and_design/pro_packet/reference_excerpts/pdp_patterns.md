# PDP Patterns
- **Product Gallery**: High-end e-commerce mix of images, video, detail shots, dimensions.
- **Watch It In Action**: Real usage demonstration (Lovevery/Little Partners).
- **Safety & Materials**: Truthful, non-exaggerated specs (Lovevery/Little Partners).
- **Room Fit**: Dimensions and room proportions (Nestig/Crate & Kids).
- **Giftability**: Highlighting baby shower/first birthday suitability (Lalo/Pottery Barn Kids).
- **Bundles**: e.g., Kitchen + Bakery, Rocker + Activity Cube (Milton & Goose).
- **FAQ**: Age, dimension, assembly, cleaning (Shopify/Apple HIG).
