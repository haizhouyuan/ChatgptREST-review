# Labebe AI Design Studio - Design Principles

Demo purpose: show a controlled Paperclip workflow that turns local evidence-gated signals into product concept and launch-test assets.

Design principles:

- Child-safe first: any safety, stability, pinch, tipping, small-part, or material concern becomes a review gate.
- Parent clarity: concepts should communicate storage, cleaning, assembly, and age-fit questions clearly.
- Compact home fit: prioritize modularity, foldability, and small-space usability where supported by sample signals.
- Warm play value: keep products imaginative and approachable without overclaiming developmental outcomes.
- Review-ready, not production-ready: concepts are artifacts for human review, not final engineering releases.
