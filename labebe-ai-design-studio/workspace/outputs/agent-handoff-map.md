## Decision Chain

1. The Data Truth Guard classifies source labels and blocks unsupported claims before product work starts.
2. The Radar and VOC agents translate sample pain into requirements without calling it market proof.
3. Design Strategy creates multiple routes, then Design Director chooses one direction and records the rejected risks.
4. DFM/Safety converts attractive design claims into stop/go gates.
5. Concept-to-Market writes only draft assets with visible source labels and blocked claims.
6. Demo Producer presents the outcome first, then uses Paperclip evidence to prove the closed loop.
