# 90-Second Boss Demo Script

## 0-20 seconds: Business hook

We are not showing another prompt demo. This is a governed product-development line for Labebe: one board, nine agents, ten epic issues, and a Data Truth smoke case. The idea starts with a compact-kitchen parent pain point and immediately enters policy, evidence, design, DFM, and launch gates.

## 20-45 seconds: Product story

Open the SpaceSmart Learning Tower path. Data Truth marks review and competitor rows as demo samples. The radar agent turns those rows into opportunity clusters. VOC turns compact storage and cleaning friction into a concept brief. Design Strategy generates four routes and selects Fold-Flat Pantry for review.

## 45-70 seconds: Governance as the wow point

Show that the system refuses to overclaim. DFM blocks tipping, pinch, small-part, cost, and certification assumptions behind engineering review. Concept-to-Market still creates PDP, Amazon A+, TikTok, Meta, Google image, and email drafts, but every asset remains draft/demo with no external account action.

## 70-90 seconds: Proof

Close on the evidence. `LAB-0` through `LAB-9` are in review, `LAB-SMOKE-001` is done, active runs are zero, secret scan is zero, and every artifact is linked from the board. The demo is memorable because it shows not only generation, but controlled generation that a business can trust.
